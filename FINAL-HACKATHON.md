# Saarthi Hackathon Final

## Judge journey
1. Add Data
2. See what is happening
3. Understand why
4. Decide with the Digital Twin
5. Act on a clear next step

## UX highlights
- Talk to Saarthi is above the journey flow.
- Large SEE → UNDERSTAND → DECIDE → ACT typography.
- Prominent Excel/CSV upload CTA.
- Saarthi guide/avatar and light formal humour.
- English, Hindi and Gujarati UI switching.
- Evidence-backed investigation and what-if simulation.
- External web Research is removed from the judge-facing product.
